# Prompts

## Executive Summary Prompt

The AI summary feature generates a short, business-focused explanation of audit results.

---

## Main Prompt

```txt
You are generating an executive summary for a startup AI spend audit.

Inputs:
- yearly AI spend
- estimated waste percentage
- optimization recommendations
- potential savings

Requirements:
- keep the summary under 120 words
- use a professional and concise tone
- explain the most important optimization opportunities
- avoid exaggerated or unrealistic claims
- ensure it is understandable for non-technical founders
- focus on cost efficiency and operational clarity
