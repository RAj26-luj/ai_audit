# Economics

This document outlines the expected unit economics, distribution assumptions, and revenue potential for StackAudit.

The goal is to provide a realistic, directional view of how the product could generate value in early-stage B2B SaaS conditions.

---

## Value of a Converted Customer

Credex is positioned around helping teams reduce AI infrastructure and tooling costs.

A typical successful optimization outcome is expected to generate:

- $500–$2000 in monthly savings per customer

Credex revenue is assumed to come from either:
- margins on AI infrastructure usage
- enterprise partnerships
- value-based pricing tied to savings generated
