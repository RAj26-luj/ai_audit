# Economics

## What A Converted Lead Could Be Worth

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits.

Assume:
- average company saves $500–$2000/month through optimization
- Credex earns revenue through margin on infrastructure credits or enterprise partnerships

If a converted customer generates even:
- $300/month in gross profit for Credex
- and stays active for 12 months

then estimated customer value becomes:

```txt
$300 × 12 = $3600 LTV
```

Even if these assumptions are directionally rough, the value of a successful B2B lead is still meaningful.

## Conversion Funnel Assumptions

Example funnel:

```txt
1000 visitors
↓
250 completed audits (25%)
↓
60 captured leads (24%)
↓
12 consultation calls (20%)
↓
3 paying customers (25%)
```

If each customer is worth roughly:

```txt
$3600 LTV
```

then:

```txt
3 × $3600 = $10,800
```

from 1000 visitors.

---

## What Makes This Profitable

The economics work if:
- audit generation remains cheap
- lead capture stays high
- reports generate trust
- savings opportunities are large enough to motivate action

The product becomes especially efficient if:
- users share audits publicly
- organic distribution compounds
- founders use it before budget reviews

## Path To $1M ARR

To reach:

```txt
$1,000,000 ARR
```

Credex would roughly need:

```txt
~278 customers paying $300/month
```

or:

```txt
~139 customers paying $600/month
```

That becomes realistic if:
- the audit tool consistently generates inbound leads
- conversion quality remains high
- the product becomes known inside startup and AI engineering communities

The biggest requirement is strong distribution, not necessarily complex technology.
