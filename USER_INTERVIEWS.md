# User Interviews & External Feedback

Due to the assignment timeline, formal structured user interviews were limited.

However, early external validation was conducted through direct outreach to professionals and startup-focused engineering communities.

---

# Outreach Message Used

A feedback request was shared asking reviewers to evaluate:

- overlapping AI subscription problems
- AI tooling usage patterns
- optimization usefulness
- dashboard clarity
- potential product value

The outreach focused on startup and engineering team workflows.

---

# External Feedback Received

One reviewer responded after testing the StackAudit demo.

## Key feedback themes

### Positive validation

- AI subscription overlap is viewed as a real and growing problem
- Cost optimization visibility was considered valuable for startups
- The dashboard was described as clean and easy to navigate
- The overall problem statement was considered highly relevant

### Suggested improvements

The reviewer suggested future improvements including:

- deeper usage analytics
- team-level reporting
- ROI comparisons between tools
- benchmarking insights
- recommendations based on company size and use cases

---

# Product Insights Learned

The feedback reinforced several assumptions behind StackAudit:

- startups increasingly lack visibility into total AI spend
- overlapping AI tooling is becoming common
- optimization recommendations are valuable even before advanced analytics
- benchmarking and ROI reporting are strong future expansion areas

---

# Current limitations

The current validation sample size is still small and not statistically meaningful.

The focus during Round 2 was prioritizing:
- shipping a working end-to-end product
- validating core workflow usefulness
- testing re-audit infrastructure reliability

instead of conducting extensive user research.

---

# Future Research Plan

Next validation steps would include:

- interviews with startup founders
- interviews with engineering managers
- usage-pattern analysis across teams
- willingness-to-pay validation
